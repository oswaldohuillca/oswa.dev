# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: recruiters and companies evaluating Oswaldo for a role, plus freelance clients looking to hire him for a site or app. They arrive with little time and decide whether to contact him. Developers arriving through his open-source work are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose
oswa.dev is the portfolio of Oswaldo Roberto Huillca Huamani ("Oswa"), a full stack web developer in Peru. Success means a qualified visitor understands what he builds and contacts him or downloads his CV.

## Positioning
He builds his own tools, not only sites: open-source software such as Dockfly, Forja, FormifyPhp and Tailwind CSS Calc sits alongside client and company work.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Astro 5 static site with Tailwind CSS v4, Markdown project collection, dev.to articles fetched at build time, and light and dark themes. The site language is Spanish. WCAG 2.2 AA fixes, navigation and the 404 page already in the working tree must be preserved.

Undecided: career start date (the site says 2021, the CV says 2019), whether Multivía joins the experience list, and the LinkedIn URL.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Oswa dev". Direct, first-person Spanish voice. No logo or other binding visual identity.

## Evidence on Hand
- Open source (github.com/oswaldohuillca): Dockfly (Go + React panel for publishing apps behind Traefik with automatic SSL, one ~54 MB image), Forja (`src/content/project/forja-fields.md`), FormifyPhp, Tailwind CSS Calc, UnoCSS preset calc, WP Dev Kit Started.
- Sites with screenshots: Singulart Apartments, Zutun.io (`public/images/project/`).
- Experience entries in `src/pages/index.astro`; portrait at `public/images/oswaldo-retrato.webp`; CV at `public/CV-Oswaldo.pdf`.
- Absent, must not be fabricated: testimonials, client logos, business results, download counts. Star counts are low and are not proof to feature.

## Product Principles
- Show the working thing before describing it.
- Every claim traces to a repo, a site or a real entry.
- A visitor can reach contact or the CV from any point in seconds.
- Own tools lead; client sites support them.
